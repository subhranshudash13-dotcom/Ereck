ABOUT US

Where Technology Meets Curiosity

Electroreck is the official electronics and technical club committed to empowering students through practical engineering, collaborative innovation, and real-world problem solving.

From embedded systems and robotics to sound-circuit synthesis and high-energy prototyping challenges, we foster an environment where ideas transform into tangible impact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about event participation, registration passes, eligibility, or joining the club? Find quick answers below.

All undergraduate and postgraduate students of NIT Kurukshetra across all branches (ECE, CSE, EE, Mechanical, Civil, etc.) and all academic years are eligible to participate in our workshops, guest lectures, and intra-college showcases.

Selected flagship hackathons, national technical symposiums, and tech-sports championships are also open to registered university students across India.

The vast majority of our workshops, bootcamps, and introductory hardware showcases are completely free of charge for all students.

For specialized hackathons or intensive hands-on workshops requiring dedicated hardware kits (e.g., microcontrollers, sensors, breadboards) or external industry certifications, any nominal material fee is explicitly stated on the event details page.

As soon as you submit the online registration form, a verified digital Registration Pass is generated on your screen with a unique tracking code (such as ER-2026-00101).

You can save a screenshot or print this ticket directly. Presenting your unique Pass ID at the registration desk ensures rapid check-in on the day of the event.

Yes! You are welcome to register for multiple events across our Technical Workshops, Cultural Showcases, and Tech-Sports championships, provided their schedules do not overlap.

Each event manages its own seating quota independently, so please ensure you submit a registration for each individual session you wish to attend.

Not at all! ELECTRORECK workshops cater to all skill levels. Our introductory sessions start from scratch with step-by-step guidance on breadboarding, microcontroller programming, and circuit debugging.

Senior club coordinators and student mentors will be by your side to guide you through every laboratory experiment.

Yes. All participants who attend the sessions and verify their attendance receive an official digital Certificate of Participation issued by ELECTRORECK, NIT Kurukshetra.

Winners and top performers in hackathons and competitive tracks receive Certificates of Merit, trophies, tech gear, and cash awards from our prize pools.

ELECTRORECK conducts annual inductions at the beginning of each semester for passionate first- and second-year students.

We recruit across several specialized wings: Hardware & IoT, Web & App Engineering, Event Management & Logistics, Creative Design & Media, and Corporate Sponsorship. Keep an eye on our portal and campus notice boards for audition announcements!
